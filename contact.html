Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries & Visiting Information

Clear answers regarding appointments, session attire, arrival guidelines, and digital health records.

How do I schedule an initial physician consultation?

You can submit your consultation inquiry using the direct intake form on this page, call our admissions desk at +1 (555) 732-8483, or request an appointment through our digital Patient Portal. Our clinical concierge reviews all inquiries within 24 hours to schedule an unhurried 60-minute intake.

What should I wear or bring to my first appointment?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting organic cotton clothing suitable for gentle movement and mobility assessment. If you have recent medical summaries, blood work, or doctor notes from the past 12 months, you are welcome to bring them or upload them in advance through your patient dashboard.

Can I visit the sanctuary grounds without a scheduled treatment?

To preserve acoustic stillness, low sensory stimulation, and privacy for patients in recovery, campus visits are by appointment only. Prospective guests are invited to attend our complimentary Guided Sanctuary Campus Tour every Saturday at 10:00 AM (prior reservation required).

Are all therapies conducted by qualified, licensed professionals?

Yes, absolutely. Every therapy session is conducted or overseen by certified naturopathic doctors (BNYS / MD) and certified yoga therapists (C-IAYT). Treatment plans are strictly non-invasive, evidence-based, and calibrated to your personal vital capacity.

How can I track my appointments, treatment plans, and receipts?

All confirmed appointments, customized circadian recommendations, and itemized billing receipts are securely stored within our digital Patient Portal. You can access and download your records anytime at portal.verdantlifewellness.org.
